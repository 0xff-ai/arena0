## Owner decisions needed

1. **`SessionStarted` termination (§3.4/§4.1).** Choose the architecture's any-event effect promise (recommended: permit `SessionEnd`/`SessionAbort` at start) or explicitly restrict it. Signing remains local-only by existing architecture.
2. **Deferred broadcast (§3.5).** Keep the documented capability (recommended until the promise changes), or forbid broadcasts from agreed events and delete the successor path. The latter changes accepted guest behavior even though shipped programs do not use it.
3. **Projection isolation (§3.10).** Keep fresh instances (recommended) or revise architecture for resident read-only calls with full rollback guarantees. Outcome JSON can be assessed separately.
4. **Terminal plus timer (§4.3).** Document rejection (recommended) or specify timer discard/retention semantics on a terminal transition.
5. **Identity switching (K1).** Treat Host identity as immutable and delete the misleading dead `set_active` (recommended), or fund a lifecycle-aware rotation feature. The current SQLite Host binding makes a direct setter call unsafe.
